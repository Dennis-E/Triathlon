Export Apple Health data

  1. Open the Health app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap your profile picture or initials in the upper-right corner.
  3. Choose Export All Health Data, then confirm Export.
  4. Save the original ZIP to Files or transfer it to your computer. Keep its contents unchanged; do not extract or repackage it.
  5. Return to TriAnalytica, choose Import Apple Health, and select that one ZIP file.

Only running, cycling and swimming workouts are imported. Routes and optional measurements are available only when the export contains compatible data. Sleep, body measurements, ECGs and other clinical records do not become workouts.

Selected health files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded or stored by TriAnalytica. The app still requests libraries, map resources and its analysis counter. Large exports may take several minutes; keep the page open.

Use a modern desktop browser over HTTP or HTTPS. If processing reaches a safety or browser resource limit, the previous dashboard remains available. Apple may change menu labels in future iOS versions.
