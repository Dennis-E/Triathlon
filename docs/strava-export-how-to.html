How to create a Strava data export

Back to dashboard

Follow these steps to generate your Strava export and use it with TriAnalytics.

  1. Open Strava and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings and choose Download Your Account.
  3. Click Request Download.
  4. Wait for Strava to email you a link to your data export.
  5. Download the ZIP file from the email and return to the dashboard.
  6. Click Strava Ingest and select the ZIP file you downloaded.

Note: Your data is processed locally in your browser. No personal training files are uploaded by this dashboard.
