TriAnalytica is now in alpha

September 24, 2026 · First published update

Today I am happy to announce the alpha launch of TriAnalytica. The platform is a way to explore your training history and turn your Strava data into useful views of your endurance journey.

I am very happy if this is also interesting for fellow athletes. Building this platform is a learning process, and I would be extremely happy to receive your feedback, suggestions, and wishes as it grows.

Thank you for taking a look at TriAnalytica and for helping shape what it can become.
