## Review Rules

- Keep only derived visualization images in `assets/previews/`.
- Do not include the private ZIP, raw CSV/GPX/FIT content, account identifiers, personal names, or exact identifying routes.
- Approve an asset only after its chart purpose and readable labels are confirmed.
- Equipment and Equipment Timeline labels are intentionally covered to avoid publishing
	personal gear names.
- The published Heatmap asset preserves the route-density visual language but removes
	exact geography and location information.
- The corrected Heatmap intentionally uses a labeled Rheinland-area context while
  replacing exact personal route geometry.
