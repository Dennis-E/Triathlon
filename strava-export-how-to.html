How to create a Strava data export

Back to dashboard

Follow these steps to generate your Strava export and use it with this dashboard.

  1. Open Strava Account Settings and sign in.
  2. Scroll down to Download or Delete Your Account.
  3. Click Get Started under Download Request.
  4. Request your archive and confirm the process in Strava.
  5. Wait for Strava to send you an email with the download link (can take minutes to hours).
  6. Download the ZIP archive from the email link.
  7. Go back to this dashboard and click Strava Ingest.
  8. Select the ZIP file you downloaded and wait until the import is complete.

Note: Your data is processed locally in your browser. No personal training files are uploaded by this dashboard.
